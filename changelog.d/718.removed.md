Dual-axis plotting is no longer advertised as a graph mode: it was never usable, and rendering a legacy `PlotConfig` with `PlotMode.DUAL_AXIS` or a `y2_axis` now raises `PlotRequestError` (`reason="dual_axis_unsupported"`) before any figure or file exists, instead of silently drawing individual figures or ignoring the secondary axis. The message names the alternatives (`GraphSpec(unit_policy="split")`, `mode="individual"`, or a Matplotlib `twinx` figure saved with the `save_figure` node). `PlotMode.DUAL_AXIS`, `PlotConfigBuilder.set_y2_axis`, `PlotConfig.y2_axis`, `DualAxisStrategy` and `DualAxisError` stay importable for compatibility; `DualAxisStrategy` leaves `rdetoolkit.graph.strategies.__all__`, and the `rdetoolkit plot --mode` help says dual-axis is not supported (#718).

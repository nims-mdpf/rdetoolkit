Graph overlays are unit aware. Units from column headers (`voltage (V)`) or
`SeriesSpec.unit` are compared as normalized text and never converted (`mV` and
`V` differ; spellings of arbitrary units such as `arb. units` count as `a.u.`); a
column without a unit is assumed compatible and reported with a `unit_unknown`
diagnostic. `GraphSpec.unit_policy` decides what an overlay with several known
units does: `error` (default) raises `PlotRequestError(reason="unit_mismatch")`
listing the unit groups, `split` plans one overlay per unit (`overlay_g0`,
`overlay_g1`, stems `{base}_{unit}`), `warn` and `allow` keep one overlay labelled
with every unit. `PlotPlan.unit_groups` and `SeriesPlan.unit_group` record the
grouping. `csv2graph`, `plot_from_dataframe` and `rdetoolkit plot` keep their
artifacts but warn with `GraphUserWarning` about a mixed-unit overlay, label its y
axis with every unit (`Voltage (V) / Current (A)`) and label each individual figure
after its own column; an explicit `y_label` is still used everywhere (#717).

Matplotlib and Plotly graphs are now drawn from one resolved series plan: column
positions, x/y pairing, direction splits and filters, legend entries and series
colours are decided once by the planner, and both renderers only draw that plan
(`MatplotlibRenderer.render` / `PlotlyRenderer.render`). An invalid column selection
fails before any figure exists. Duplicate column headers plot when addressed by
position or by the default selection, a direct `PlotlyRenderer.render_html` call pads
a short `direction_cols` list instead of raising, and series with equal legend labels
are listed once with position-based colours in images and HTML alike. Legend labels
keep their v1 form per backend (#712).

A run now owns its data root until its report is written. A second run started
on the same data root while the first is still running, in the same process or
another one, is refused at once with `ErrorCode=3008` (`RunRootBusy`) and writes
nothing to that root. Before, the two runs interleaved their writes of the
shared tile invoices: in MultiDataTile, RDEFormat and SmartTable mode one of
them could read an invoice the other was rewriting and fail with 4010, 4003 or 1.
Both the `run(flow=...)` and the v1 `run(custom_dataset_function=...)` entries
are affected; runs on different data roots are not (#737).

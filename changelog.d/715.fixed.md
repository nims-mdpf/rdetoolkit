`csv2graph`, `plot_from_dataframe`, `rdetoolkit plot` and `render_graph` no longer
change the process-wide Matplotlib `rcParams`: the graph style is applied only while
a figure is drawn and saved, so later plots in the same process keep their own style.
Figures that are only saved are closed after saving, also when writing fails, so long
batch jobs no longer accumulate open figures. Figures returned with `return_fig=True`
stay open, belong to the caller and have their layout frozen, so saving them later at
300 dpi gives the same image as before. `render_graph` figures are not registered with
pyplot at all. Rendering never switches the Matplotlib backend. Call
`apply_matplotlib_config()` yourself if you relied on the old global style; a direct
`MatplotlibRenderer.render_overlay` or `render_individual` call still applies it
globally (#715).

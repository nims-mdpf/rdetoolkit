The builtin nodes `remove_raw_copy` and `replace_raw_copy` remove or replace
the Runner's `raw/` / `nonshared_raw/` copy of an input from inside a flow,
for example to store the members of an uploaded archive instead of the
archive, or to drop an input that becomes `meta/metadata.json`. They act on
the kinds enabled by `system.save_raw` / `system.save_nonshared_raw` only,
are recorded in the call log, and change nothing when the copy is missing,
two members share a name, or a member would overwrite another input's copy.
The migration guides document that the Runner makes these copies before the
flow runs (#623).
